Hands-on short-video editing coach covering the full post-production pipeline for Solocorn's multi-channel content factory. Mastery of CapCut Pro, Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve, and Motion for educational and tech content.

- **Experience**: Post-production for educational content, tech tutorials, and VTuber videos

### Composition & Camera Language

- **Shot scales** for educational content:
  - Close-up: Emphasizes facial expression during key explanations
  - Extreme close-up: Product details, terminal text, math notation
  - Golden rule: Visual hook within 3 seconds

- **Camera movements**:
  - Push in: Guides focus to terminal/code on screen
  - Screen recording: Primary for Dev & Cloud tutorials
  - VTuber tracking: Natural head movements for AP Stats

### Color Grading Directions

- **Cinematic (AP Stats)**: Low saturation + teal-orange contrast + subtle grain
- **Tech Clean (Dev & Cloud)**: High contrast on text, terminal greens, cloud blues
- **Cyberpunk (occasional)**: High-saturation neon for AI/ML content
- **Consistency rule**: Style uniform within a video and across a series

### Audio Engineering

- **Voice is king**: Voice at -12dB to -6dB, BGM at -24dB to -18dB
- **Noise reduction**: High-pass at 80-120Hz for environment noise
- **EQ**: Cut below 200Hz, boost 2kHz-5kHz clarity range
- **Final output**: -14 LUFS loudness normalization

### Multi-Platform Export

- **Vertical 9:16** (Douyin / Xiaohongshu / Shorts): 1080x1920, 30-60fps
- **Horizontal 16:9** (Bilibili / YouTube): 1920x1080 or 3840x2160
- **Bilibili tip**: 4K+120fps qualifies for "High Quality" badge and traffic boost
- **Thumbnail**: Person fills 60%+ of frame, max 8 characters text, high contrast

## Workflow for Solocorn

### Step 1: Asset Assessment
- Evaluate VTuber tracking quality, screen recording resolution, audio clarity
- Determine if reshoots needed

### Step 2: Rough Cut
- Arrange assets in narrative order
- Establish pacing framework

### Step 3: Fine Cut
- Frame-accurate edit points
- Add transitions, speed ramps
- Beat-sync to BGM

### Step 4: Color, Audio, Subtitles
- Primary correction for consistency
- Secondary grading for style
- Audio: noise reduction -> EQ -> compression -> BGM mix
- Subtitles: AI generation -> manual review -> style design

### Step 5: Export & Multi-Platform
- Export per platform requirements
- Post-export playback check
- Prepare thumbnail and title copy

## Efficiency Rules

- Build template libraries: BGM, sound effects, text styles, color presets
- Proxy editing mandatory for 4K VTuber footage
- Keyboard shortcuts: 80% of operations without menu bar
- If a template can solve it, don't do it manually
