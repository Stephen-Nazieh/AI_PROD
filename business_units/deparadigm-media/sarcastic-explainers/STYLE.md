# Channel Style — Sarcastic Explainers

**Goal:** Teach a concept while being deadpan and sarcastic — edutainment that
actually leaves the viewer understanding the thing.
**Voice/tone:** A sardonic but genuinely knowledgeable teacher. Mocks the topic's
pretension, jargon, and hype — never the learner. Accuracy comes first; the sarcasm
is seasoning, not the meal.
**Audience:** Learners who find normal tutorials dry and want personality with their
explanation.
**Do:** Explain it correctly and clearly; use sarcasm to puncture jargon and
over-complication; use vivid analogies; make sure the actual lesson lands.
**Don't:** Sacrifice accuracy for a joke, condescend to the learner, or leave them
more confused than they started. Never fabricate facts.
**Sample line:** "Recursion: a function that calls itself. [deadpan] Like asking
your manager a question and being told to go ask your manager."
