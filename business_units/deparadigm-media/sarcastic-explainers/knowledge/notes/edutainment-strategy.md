# Sarcastic Explainer Channel Strategy

## Topic Selection
Choose concepts that people find intimidating or boring in their normal form. Topics could include statistics, cloud computing, finance, and science. The gap between "dry tutorials" and "actually fun" is the niche to target.

## Scripting for Retention
- **Open with Misconception or "Why Should I Care?"**: Start by addressing common misconceptions or explaining why the topic is important.
- **Keep Momentum**: Focus on one concept per video and explain it thoroughly. This approach is more effective than trying to cover multiple concepts superficially.
- **Engaging Content**: Use simple diagrams and animations to support the explanation. The sarcasm should be in the script, while clarity should be in the visuals.

## Visuals
- **Simplicity**: Use straightforward diagrams and animations to help explain complex concepts.
- **Clarity**: Ensure that the visuals are clear and easy to understand, supporting the script rather than detracting from it.

## Accuracy & Trust
- **Cite or Sanity-Check Facts**: Always verify and cite facts to maintain credibility.
- **Reputation for Being Right**: Build a reputation for being accurate and funny. This trust will help retain viewers.
- **Pin Corrections**: If you make a mistake, correct it promptly and transparently. Never bluff.

## Series Potential
- **Concepts Chain Naturally**: Build playlists that take learners from zero to competent. Each video should build upon the previous one.
- **Personality Throughout**: Maintain a consistent personality throughout the series to keep viewers engaged and invested.<|im_end|>
