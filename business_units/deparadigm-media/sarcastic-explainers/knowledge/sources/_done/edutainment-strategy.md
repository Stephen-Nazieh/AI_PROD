Sarcastic Explainer Channel Strategy

Topic selection: concepts people find intimidating or boring in their normal form
(stats, cloud, finance, science) — the gap between "dry tutorial" and "actually
fun" is the niche.
Scripting for retention: open on the misconception or the "why should I care";
keep momentum; one concept per video done thoroughly beats five done shallowly.
Visuals: simple diagrams/animations that carry the explanation; the sarcasm is in
the script, the clarity is in the visuals.
Accuracy & trust: cite or sanity-check facts; a reputation for being right (and
funny) is the moat. Pin corrections; never bluff.
Series potential: concepts chain naturally ("Stats, but make it bearable") — build
playlists that take a learner from zero to competent with personality throughout.
