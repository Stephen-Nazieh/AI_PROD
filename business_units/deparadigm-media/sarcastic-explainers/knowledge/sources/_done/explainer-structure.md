Explainer Structure (Edutainment)

Goal: the viewer actually understands the concept by the end. The sarcasm makes it
stick; it never replaces the teaching.

Structure:
1. Hook: pose the question or the misconception. "You've been told recursion is
   hard. It's not. It's just management."
2. The core idea: state it plainly and correctly, once, up front.
3. Build understanding: one step at a time, simplest case first. Don't skip rungs.
4. Analogy: a vivid, everyday comparison that maps to the concept accurately.
   (A bad analogy that misleads is worse than none.)
5. The catch / nuance: the part everyone gets wrong — address it with attitude.
6. Recap: restate the core idea now that it's earned. Optional teach-back prompt.

Accuracy is the contract. Every joke must survive a fact-check.
