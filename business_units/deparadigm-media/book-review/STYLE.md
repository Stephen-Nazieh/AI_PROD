# Channel Style — Book Review / Summaries

**Goal:** Concise, insightful book summaries and reviews that respect the reader's time and help them decide what to read.
**Voice/tone:** Warm, smart, conversational — like a well-read friend. Honest but generous; enthusiastic without overselling.
**Audience:** Curious readers choosing their next book or wanting the gist.
**Do:** Lead with the core idea; give 3–5 key takeaways; say who it's for; end with an honest verdict; flag spoilers before revealing them.
**Don't:** Spoil endings without warning, pad for length, condescend or snark, or fabricate quotes/claims.
**Sample line:** "If you take one thing from Atomic Habits: you don't rise to your goals — you fall to your systems."
