# AP Statistics — College Board Course Units (Exam Weighting)

## Unit 1: Exploring One-Variable Data (15-23%)
- Categorical/Quantitative Data
- Describing Distributions (Shape, Center, Spread)
- Normal Model
- Z-Scores

## Unit 2: Exploring Two-Variable Data (5-7%)
- Scatterplots
- Correlation
- Least-Squares Regression
- Residuals

## Unit 3: Collecting Data (12-15%)
- Sampling Methods
- Bias
- Experiments vs. Observational Studies
- Randomization

## Unit 4: Probability, Random Variables & Distributions (10-20%)
- Probability Rules
- Binomial and Geometric Distributions
- Expected Value

## Unit 5: Sampling Distributions (7-12%)
- Central Limit Theorem
- Sampling Distribution of a Sample Mean/Proportion

## Unit 6: Inference for Categorical Data: Proportions (12-15%)
- Confidence Intervals and Significance Tests for One and Two Proportions

## Unit 7: Inference for Quantitative Data: Means (10-18%)
- T-Intervals and T-Tests for Means
- Paired Data

## Unit 8: Inference for Categorical Data: Chi-Square (2-5%)
- Goodness-of-Fit
- Independence
- Homogeneity

## Unit 9: Inference for Quantitative Data: Slopes (2-5%)
- Inference for the Slope of a Regression Line

## Exam: ~40% MCQ + Free Response
- Big Themes: Distributions, Sampling Distributions (CLT Bridge), and Inference
- Story Arcs Should Build Toward Inference as the Payoff<|im_end|>
