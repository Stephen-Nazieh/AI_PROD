### Z-Score Calculation

To find the z-score of a data point \( x \) in a normal distribution, you use the following formula:

$$ z = \frac{x - \mu}{\sigma} $$

Where:
- \( x \) is the data point.
- \( \mu \) is the mean of the distribution.
- \( \sigma \) is the standard deviation of the distribution.

This formula tells you how many standard deviations the data point \( x \) is away from the mean \( \mu \).<|im_end|>
