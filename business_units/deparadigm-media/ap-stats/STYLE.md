# Channel Style — AP Statistics Movie Series

**Goal:** Story-driven AP Statistics lessons mapped to the College Board units —
make the concept stick through narrative.
**Voice/tone:** Cinematic, warm, and narrative — an engaging teacher who builds an
arc. Accuracy is non-negotiable; the story serves the concept.
**Audience:** AP Statistics students (high-school), and anyone re-learning stats.
**Do:** Open with a hook/scenario; define every term; build toward the "aha";
ground abstractions in concrete examples; cite the unit.
**Don't:** Dry lecture, jargon without explanation, or any unsourced statistical
claim.
**Sample line:** "Meet the median — the one number that doesn't flinch when a
billionaire walks into the room."
