# Long-Form Ambient Channel Strategy

## Format
- **Duration**: 1-10 hour single-scene loops (study/coding/sleep/rain)
- **Concept**: One strong concept per video (e.g., "Rainy Night Coding Cafe — 10 Hours")

## Titles
- **Format**: "Vibe + Use Case + Duration"
- **Example**: "Deep Focus Lofi — Coding & Studying — 10 Hours"

## Keywords
- focus
- study
- coding
- ambient
- lofi
- rain
- no ads

## Thumbnails
- **Visual**: One calm, atmospheric still of the scene
- **Text**: Minimal
- **Identity**: Consistent visual identity across the channel

## Retention/Passive Watch
- **Long-Form**: Thrives on session length and loops — viewers leave it running
- **Preview**: Front-load the most pleasant 30 seconds (the preview), then settle
- **Chapters**: Sparse for navigation

## Monetization
- **Watch-Time**: Strong ad inventory (mid-rolls spread thin so they don't jar focus — or run ad-free as a brand promise and monetize via memberships/links)
- **Playlists**: "Focus", "Sleep", "Rain" drive autoplay across the catalog

## Cost
- **Production**: Cheap (loop a short base) and evergreen — back-catalog keeps earning
- **Batch Production**: Scene variants from one pipeline<|im_end|>
