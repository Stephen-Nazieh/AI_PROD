# Deep-Focus Audio Design

## Goal
The audio should recede into the background, promoting sustained attention without engaging the listener. This is achieved by avoiding hooks, surprises, and lyrics.

## Tempo & Structure
- **Tempo:** 60-90 BPM (or beatless)
- **Structure:** Minimal-to-no melody movement; favor evolving pads, soft textures, and gentle rhythmic elements. Avoid build-ups, drops, and silence gaps that pull attention.

## Layers
1. **Harmonic Bed:** Warm pads, Rhodes, soft synth
2. **Optional Light Percussion:** Lo-fi hip-hop swing, brushed kit
3. **Noise/Ambience Layer:** Brown noise, rain, café, or nature — to mask distractions and aid concentration

## Mixing
- **Harsh Highs:** Roll off shelf above ~10kHz
- **Mud:** Tame frequencies between 200-400Hz
- **Dynamic Range:** Keep narrow with gentle compression to maintain steady volume for passive listening
- **Target LUFS:** ~-16 to -18 LUFS (quieter than music; it's a backdrop)

## Avoid
- Recognizable samples/melodies that trigger memory
- Sudden timbre changes
- Anything copyrighted
