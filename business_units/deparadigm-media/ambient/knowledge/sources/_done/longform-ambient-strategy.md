Long-Form Ambient Channel Strategy

Format: 1-10 hour single-scene loops (study/coding/sleep/rain). One strong concept
per video (e.g., "Rainy Night Coding Cafe — 10 Hours").

Titles: lead with the vibe + use case + duration: "Deep Focus Lofi — Coding &
Studying — 10 Hours". Keywords: focus, study, coding, ambient, lofi, rain, no ads.

Thumbnails: one calm, atmospheric still of the scene; minimal text; consistent
visual identity across the channel so the playlist reads as a set.

Retention/passive watch: long-form thrives on session length and loops — viewers
leave it running. Front-load the most pleasant 30s (the preview), then settle.
Chapters help navigation but keep them sparse.

Monetization: long watch-time = strong ad inventory (mid-rolls spread thin so they
don't jar focus — or run ad-free as a brand promise and monetize via memberships/
links). Playlists ("Focus", "Sleep", "Rain") drive autoplay across the catalog.

Cost: production is cheap (loop a short base) and evergreen — back-catalog keeps
earning. Batch-produce scene variants from one pipeline.
