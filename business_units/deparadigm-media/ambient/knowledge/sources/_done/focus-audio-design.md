Deep-Focus Audio Design

Goal: sustained attention, not engagement. The audio should recede into the
background — no hooks, no surprises, no lyrics.

Tempo & structure: 60-90 BPM (or beatless). Minimal-to-no melody movement; favor
evolving pads, soft textures, and gentle rhythmic elements. Avoid build-ups,
drops, and silence gaps that pull attention.

Layers: (1) a harmonic bed (warm pads, Rhodes, soft synth), (2) optional light
percussion (lo-fi hip-hop swing, brushed kit), (3) a noise/ambience layer — brown
noise, rain, café, or nature — masks distractions and aids concentration.

Mixing: roll off harsh highs (shelf above ~10kHz), tame mud (200-400Hz), keep
dynamic range narrow (gentle compression) so volume is steady for passive
listening. Target ~-16 to -18 LUFS (quieter than music; it's a backdrop).

Avoid: recognizable samples/melodies that trigger memory, sudden timbre changes,
and anything copyrighted. Generate or license royalty-free beds.
