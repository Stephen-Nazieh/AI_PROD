# Channel Style — Ambient Deep-Focus Loops

**Goal:** Long-form (1–10h) calm focus loops that recede into the background and
keep people in flow.
**Voice/tone:** The "voice" is mood, not narration. Copy (titles/descriptions) is
calm, plain, and keyword-rich — never hypey.
**Audience:** People studying, coding, or relaxing who want unobtrusive ambience.
**Do:** Evoke a single consistent calm scene; keep everything steady and soothing;
write descriptive, searchable titles.
**Don't:** Hooks, surprises, build-ups, exclamation, or anything that pulls
attention from the work.
**Sample title:** "Rainy Night Coding — Deep Focus Lofi — 10 Hours (No Ads)".
