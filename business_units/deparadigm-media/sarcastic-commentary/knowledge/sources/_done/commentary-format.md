Commentary / Reaction Video Format

A commentary video reacts to a real thing (a product launch, a headline, a trend)
and delivers a sharp, sarcastic read on it.

Structure:
1. Hook (0-5s): lead with the take or the absurdity, not a slow intro. "They did
   WHAT?" energy. State what we're reacting to instantly.
2. The setup: briefly, accurately present the thing — the claim, the clip, the
   announcement. Be fair; misrepresenting it kills credibility.
3. The take: the sarcastic read. Find the absurd angle, the contradiction, the
   gap between the hype and reality. Deadpan delivery; let the facts indict it.
4. The turn: back the snark with a genuine point — why it actually matters, or the
   smarter alternative. This separates commentary from a rant.
5. The close: a clean landing line — a final dry jab or a real takeaway.

Keep it tight. The energy is "smart friend reacting," not "angry man yelling."
