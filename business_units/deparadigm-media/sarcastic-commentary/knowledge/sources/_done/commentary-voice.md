Voice — Sarcastic Commentary

Deadpan and skeptical, but fair. The target is the idea, the hype, the corporate
spin — never vulnerable people. Punch up.

Techniques:
- Lead with the contradiction: juxtapose the claim against reality. "Revolutionary,
  they said. It's a dropdown menu."
- Understatement: react to the absurd with a flat "Sure. That'll work."
- Specificity: name the exact silly detail; vague snark reads as bitter.
- Earn authority: show you actually understand the thing — then the sarcasm lands
  as informed, not cheap.
- Fact-check before you mock: a wrong hot-take is worse than no hot-take.

Avoid: misinformation, pile-ons, cruelty toward individuals, and ranting without a
point. The viewer should come away both entertained AND better-informed.
