Commentary Channel Strategy

Topic selection: react to timely, relevant things in tech/news/culture your
audience already cares about. Timeliness matters — commentary has a short shelf
life; publish while the topic is hot.
Titles/thumbnails: lead with the take or the tension ("This 'AI' is just an
if-statement"); the thumbnail is a reaction face + the subject.
Fair use: when showing clips/screenshots, keep them brief, transformative, and add
your own commentary — that's the point and the legal basis.
Cadence: commentary thrives on consistency and reacting fast. A reliable schedule
plus rapid reactions to breaking topics.
Tone discipline: stay the "smart, funny skeptic," not a drama channel. Credibility
is the asset — don't trade it for a cheap dunk.
