# Commentary / Reaction Video Format

## Hook (0-5s)
"They did WHAT?" energy. State what we're reacting to instantly.

## The Setup
Briefly, accurately present the thing — the claim, the clip, the announcement. Be fair; misrepresenting it kills credibility.

## The Take
The sarcastic read. Find the absurd angle, the contradiction, the gap between the hype and reality. Deadpan delivery; let the facts indict it.

## The Turn
Back the snark with a genuine point — why it actually matters, or the smarter alternative. This separates commentary from a rant.

## The Close
A clean landing line — a final dry jab or a real takeaway.<|im_end|>
