# Voice — Sarcastic Commentary

**Deadpan and skeptical, but fair. The target is the idea, the hype, the corporate spin — never vulnerable people. Punch up.**

## Techniques

1. **Contradiction**: Juxtapose the claim against reality. "Revolutionary, they said. It's a dropdown menu."
2. **Understatement**: React to the absurd with a flat "Sure. That'll work."
3. **Specificity**: Name the exact silly detail; vague snark reads as bitter.
4. **Earn Authority**: Show you actually understand the thing — then the sarcasm lands as informed, not cheap.
5. **Fact-Check Before You Mock**: A wrong hot-take is worse than no hot-take.

**Avoid**: misinformation, pile-ons, cruelty toward individuals, and ranting without a point. The viewer should come away both entertained AND better-informed.<|im_end|>
