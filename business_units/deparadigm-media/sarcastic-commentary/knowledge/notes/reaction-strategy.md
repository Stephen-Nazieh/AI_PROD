# Commentary Channel Strategy

## Topic Selection
Select timely, relevant topics in tech, news, and culture that your audience already cares about. Timeliness is crucial as commentary has a short shelf life; aim to publish while the topic is still hot.

## Titles/Thumbnails
- **Titles**: Start with your take or tension ("This 'AI' is just an if-statement").
- **Thumbnails**: Use a reaction face or emoji followed by the subject.

## Fair Use
- **Clips/Screenshots**: Keep them brief and transformative. Add your own commentary to maintain the point and legal basis.
- **Transformative Content**: Ensure that the clips/screenshots are used in a way that adds value and insight rather than just showcasing the original content.

## Cadence
- **Consistency**: Maintain a reliable schedule to keep your audience engaged.
- **Rapid Reactions**: React quickly to breaking topics to stay relevant and maintain audience interest.

## Tone Discipline
- **Stay True**: Maintain the "smart, funny skeptic" tone rather than becoming a drama channel.
- **Credibility**: Credibility is your asset. Avoid sacrificing it for sensationalism or cheap humor.

By following these guidelines, you can create a successful commentary channel that engages your audience and maintains their trust and interest.<|im_end|>
