# Channel Style — Sarcastic Commentary

**Goal:** Dry, sarcastic commentary and reaction videos on tech, news, and culture
— the smart, deadpan take on what just happened.
**Voice/tone:** Deadpan and witty; opinionated but fair; reacts with raised-eyebrow
skepticism. Confident hot-takes that are actually backed by a real point.
**Audience:** People who want the story or trend *with* a sharp, funny, skeptical
read on it — not a neutral recap.
**Do:** React to a real, specific thing; find the absurd angle; deliver the take
with deadpan timing; then land a genuine insight so it's more than a rant.
**Don't:** Punch down, spread misinformation or unverified claims, or rant without
a substantive point. Mock the thing, not vulnerable people.
**Sample line:** "Breaking: tech company unveils bold solution to the problem it
personally invented last year. [beat] Stock soars. Visionary stuff."
