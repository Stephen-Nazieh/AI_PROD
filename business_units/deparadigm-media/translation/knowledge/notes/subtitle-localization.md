# Subtitle & Localization Standards

## Reading Speed
- Target: ≤17 characters/second (CPS); hard cap ~21 CPS.
- Min duration: ~1s, max ~7s per cue.

## Line Length
- ≤42 characters/line, max 2 lines.
- Break at clause boundaries, never mid-word or after an article.

## Spanish
- Expect longer text — condense without losing meaning.
- Use formal vs informal register consistently (usted vs tú) per channel voice.

## Mandarin
- No spaces between words; ~14-16 full-width characters/line.
- Mind line breaks at sense units.
- Simplified vs Traditional must match the target market (Mainland = Simplified, Taiwan/HK = Traditional).

## Sync
- Subtitle in/out within ~150ms of speech.
- Never let a cue outlast the shot.

## Numbers, Names, and On-Screen Text
- Localize numerals and provide translated on-screen text as forced narrative subtitles.<|im_end|>
