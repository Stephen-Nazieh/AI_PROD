Subtitle & Localization Standards

Reading speed: target <=17 characters/second (CPS); hard cap ~21 CPS. Min duration
~1s, max ~7s per cue.
Line length: <=42 characters/line, max 2 lines. Break at clause boundaries, never
mid-word or after an article.
Spanish: expect longer text — condense without losing meaning; use formal vs
informal register consistently (usted vs tú) per channel voice.
Mandarin: no spaces between words; ~14-16 full-width characters/line; mind line
breaks at sense units; Simplified vs Traditional must match the target market
(Mainland = Simplified, Taiwan/HK = Traditional).
Sync: subtitle in/out within ~150ms of speech; never let a cue outlast the shot.
Numbers, names, and on-screen text: localize numerals and provide translated
on-screen text as forced narrative subtitles.
