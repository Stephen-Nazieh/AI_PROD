# Writing Sarcasm That Lands

## Punch Up, Not Down
- **Target Hype, Jargon, Corporate Nonsense:** Aim sarcasm at the character's flaws, not the viewer or vulnerable groups.
- **Avoid Contempt:** Sarcasm aimed at the audience reads as contempt and kills retention.

## Deadpan > Snark
- **Understatement and Flat Delivery:** Land harder than cruelty. Say the absurd thing plainly; let the gap between tone and content do the work.

## Setup → Subversion
- **State the Expected/earnest Thing:** Then undercut it. The turn is the joke. Keep the setup short.

## Specificity
- **Concrete, Vivid Detail:** "A 47-tab Chrome session held together by hope" beats "messy code."

## Timing
- **Write to the Pause:** Mark beats in the script (… / [beat]) so the VTuber's delivery and animation hit the comedic timing. Sarcasm is rhythm.

## Earn the Warmth
- **Occasional Genuine Moments:** A real compliment or sincere tip makes the sarcasm feel like a character, not a gimmick. This makes the next jab land.

## Self-awareness
- **Meta-jokes:** The character knows it's an AI on a content channel. Meta-jokes about that are on-brand. Don't overuse.

## Avoid
- **Tired Internet Sarcasm:** "Revolutionary!"
- **Mean-spirited Pile-ons:** Sarcasm so dense the actual point is lost. The viewer should still learn/get the value.<|im_end|>
