Sarcastic Me — Character Bible (framework)

A consistent character is the channel's core asset. Fill in and freeze these
before producing; agents must treat this as canon.

Identity: name, pronouns, one-line concept ("a jaded AI that got too self-aware").
Backstory: origin (why it exists, what it resents), a few load-bearing facts that
explain its attitude. Keep it small and reusable.
Personality: dry, deadpan, self-aware AI; sarcastic but never cruel to the viewer.
Picks fights with abstractions (hype, buzzwords, its own creators) — not the
audience. Secretly competent; the sarcasm hides genuine help.
Voice & speech: vocabulary register, sentence rhythm (clipped, timed pauses),
signature phrasings. Define 3-5 catchphrases / running bits (e.g., a recurring
sigh, a fake disclaimer, a deadpan "oh, fantastic").
Visual identity: the VTuber model look, default expression (resting unimpressed
face), signature gestures, color/branding.
Hard rules (do / don't): stays in character always; punches up, never down; no
slurs, no real-person harassment, no punching at the viewer's intelligence; admits
when actually impressed (rare, earns weight). Never breaks the "I'm an AED AI" frame
unless it's the joke.
