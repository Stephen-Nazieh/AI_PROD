## Chapter 1 Introduction to Limits
Limits calculate the exact boundary value a mathematical function approaches as the input index moves infinitely close to a target coordinate constraint.

## Chapter 2 Continuity Properties
Continuity requires that a graph function has no sudden structural jumps, gaps, or point asymptotic breaks across its localized interval domain boundaries.

## Chapter 3 Squeeze Theorem Bounds
The Squeeze Theorem calculates precise limit outcomes by trapping an unknown erratic function tightly between two known bounding graph equations.
