A local-first, solo-operated AI media production studio based in China.

1. **Developer/Cloud EdTech** — Serverless GCP architectures, AWS transitions, Security+ compliance
2. **AP Statistics Movie Series** — Cinematic, story-driven multi-episode seasons
3. **Multi-Language Translation Factory** — Automated Spanish and Mandarin dubbing via XTTS v2
4. **Passive Deep-Focus Atmospheric Loops** — 10-hour coding ambience channels
